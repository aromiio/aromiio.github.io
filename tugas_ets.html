About AMUSE

AMUSE is a Seoul-inspired vegan beauty brand that creates light, clean, and effortless makeup for everyday life. With a focus on sustainability and healthier skin, AMUSE delivers products that feel fresh, simple, and naturally uplifting.

Inspired by Seoul’s vibrant lifestyle, each product is designed to bring comfort, glow, and a joyful beauty experience.

Read More

01 Vegan & Wellness Beauty

AMUSE creates clean, high-quality, and ethical beauty products with vegan certification, designed to support healthier skin and everyday comfort.

02 Sustainable Beauty Value

AMUSE continues to expand its vegan and wellness beauty values into everyday life while staying mindful of sustainability and the environment.

03 Inspired by Seoul Lifestyle

Inspired by Seoul’s lifestyle and culture, AMUSE blends naturally into daily beauty routines and helps everyone discover a refreshed version of themselves.
